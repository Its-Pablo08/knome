Posts: Quick Updates & Team Announcements

Fast, informal updates to celebrate wins, ask questions, or broadcast team news across the company.

👤 Who: Any Employee ⏱️ Time: ~30 Seconds ⚡ Approval: Instant Pass (Auto-filtered) 🏆 Reward: +2 Karma Points
1
✏️ Write & Tag
Click "Create Post", type your update, attach photos or links, and @mention teammates or add hashtags.
➔
2
⚡ Instant Auto-Check
The system checks for safe workplace words in under 1 second. Clean posts are published immediately without delay.
➔
3
📡 Live on Feed
Your update appears instantly on the company-wide feed and department stream without anyone needing to refresh!
➔
4
🎉 React & +2 Karma
Colleagues like, clap, and reply with comments. You receive +2 Karma points on your profile badge.
💡
Real Workplace Example: A project lead posts: "Great job team! MPOnline Portal Phase 1 migration completed ahead of schedule 🚀" — team members instantly comment, celebrate with claps, and stay aligned.
📄

Articles: In-Depth Guides, Best Practices & Manuals

Long-form structured guides with headings, diagrams, bullet points, and downloadable PDF attachments.

👤 Who: Any Employee / Team Lead ⏱️ Time: 5–15 Minutes ⚡ Approval: Instant Pass to Knowledge Hub 🏆 Reward: +10 Karma Points
1
📝 Rich Editor Draft
Write your guide with formatted text, headers, images, code blocks, and cover banners. Auto-saves as you type.
➔
2
🏷️ Topic & Read-Time
Select your department category (e.g. IT, Finance, HR). The system automatically calculates read-time (e.g. 4 min).
➔
3
📚 Permanent Library
Published directly to the company Knowledge Hub. Fully searchable so coworkers find answers when searching.
➔
4
🛡️ Read In-App (+10)
Staff read directly inside Knome without messy file downloads. Contributor earns +10 Karma points.
💡
Real Workplace Example: An IT senior writes: "Complete Guide: Connecting to MPOnline VPN from Home on Windows & Mac" — new joiners find it in 5 seconds instead of calling the helpdesk.
🎬

Clips: Quick 90-Second Vertical How-To Videos

Bite-sized mobile & desktop vertical video reels for screen recordings, feature demos, and fast productivity hacks.

👤 Who: Any Employee ⏱️ Time: Under 2 Minutes ⚡ Approval: Instant Pass to Reels Feed 🏆 Reward: +8 Karma Points
1
🎥 Record or Upload
Record your screen with 1 click in Knome, or upload a vertical video clip (up to 90 seconds).
➔
2
📱 Auto 9:16 Format
Knome automatically formats the video for smooth mobile & desktop reel playback and creates a video thumbnail.
➔
3
🍿 Swipeable Reels
Published immediately to the Clips feed. Employees swipe up or use keyboard arrows to learn quickly on the go.
➔
4
❤️ Double-Tap & +8
Colleagues double-tap to like, leave quick comments, and save tips. Creator is rewarded with +8 Karma points.
💡
Real Workplace Example: A finance officer records a 45-second screen capture: "How to download approved invoice vouchers in 3 clicks" — saves teammates 20 minutes of searching every day.
📖

Wiki: Company Handbook, Policies & Official SOPs

The single source of truth for company rules, standard procedures, and technical documentation with full edit history.

👤 Who: Any Employee / Team Admin ⏱️ Time: 5–10 Minutes ⚡ Approval: Instant Pass + Complete Version Tracking 🏆 Reward: +10 Karma Points
1
✍️ Write Procedure
Create or update an official SOP, policy, or onboarding checklist with step-by-step instructions.
➔
2
🕒 1-Click Version Undo
Every change is saved with who made it and when. Admins can see differences (diff) and restore old versions in 1 click!
➔
3
🗂️ Clean Folder Tree
Organized neatly into department directories so anyone can easily browse topics like chapters in a book.
➔
4
🔒 Never Lost (+10)
Institutional knowledge stays safely stored forever even when people change jobs. Author earns +10 Karma.
💡
Real Workplace Example: Operations creates the "Hardware Replacement & Laptop Request SOP". When policy changes 6 months later, the team updates it with a clear version audit trail.
📹

Videos: Official Training Courses & Masterclasses

Long-form horizontal training sessions with admin quality check, chapter timestamps, and employee note-taking.

👤 Who: Trainers, Leads & Approved Staff ⏱️ Time: 10–60 Min Course 🛡️ Approval: Mandatory Admin Clearance 🏆 Reward: +8 Karma (Post-Approval)
1
📤 Upload Video / Playlist
Upload MP4 video tutorial or add YouTube training playlist with title, category, and chapter descriptions.
➔
2
🛡️ Admin Review Gate
Admin clearance required! Pauses in Admin Console Media Approvals queue. Admin verifies quality before release.
➔
3
📺 Live on Video Hub
Once approved, video publishes to the Video Hub. Staff receive an alert and stream without buffering.
➔
4
⏱️ Chapters & +8 Karma
Employees skip directly to relevant chapters, ask questions, and uploader receives +8 Karma points.
💡
Real Workplace Example: Cybersecurity team uploads a 25-minute "Data Privacy & Phishing Defense 2026" course. The Admin reviews and approves it, making it mandatory training for 1,200 staff.
🎙️

Podcasts: Audio Broadcasts & Leadership Talks

Audio episodes and interview series employees can listen to in the background while working or commuting.

👤 Who: Leadership, HR & Voice Leads ⏱️ Time: 10–45 Min Audio 🛡️ Approval: Mandatory Admin Clearance 🏆 Reward: +8 Karma (Post-Approval)
1
🎙️ Upload Audio Episode
Host uploads MP3 audio file with episode number, cover artwork, speaker tags, and summary show-notes.
➔
2
🛡️ Admin Review Gate
Admin clearance required! Routed to Media Approvals queue for check of sound quality and corporate guidelines.
➔
3
🎧 Continuous Audio Player
Approved episode mounts in Knome's global bottom player. Plays smoothly without stopping while you browse other pages!
➔
4
⭐ Rate & +8 Karma
Employees rate episodes, bookmark series, and award the creator +8 Karma points.
💡
Real Workplace Example: Managing Director records "Monthly Leadership Address: 2026 Goals & Tech Roadmap". Employees listen with headphones while completing their morning tasks.
